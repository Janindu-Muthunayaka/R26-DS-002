Interactive Sinhala Text Reader for the Visually Impaired using OCR, NLP, AI

L0

voice

Voice interaction. Runs before everything — decides if downstream even runs.

L2

select

Quality control — picks the best photos from a burst (least blur, most vivid).

L3

segment

Splits pages via YOLO, deskews segments, and uses PaddleOCR to identify title & body regions.

L4A

title

Title extractor — CRAFT polygon detection, binarization, and RAW Tesseract OCR.

L4B

body

Body text extractor — pulls text from document bodies and paragraphs.

L4C

Correctional

The output after post OCR correction from MT5 model

L5

assemble

Aggregates titles and text per article across documents into an interactive output.

L6

generator

RAG generator — uses the session document + L0 routing to answer or summarize.

L7

personalization

Detects the spoken intent and adapts answer style, speed and pauses to each user.

L8A

listen

Captures the user's spoken Sinhala question and turns it into text.

L8B

speech

Speaks each answer aloud in Sinhala at the user's preferred speed and pause.
